# Amr Hassan

## Systems Architect | AI-Augmented Workflows

I design **governed, AI-augmented systems** with explicit lifecycles, authority boundaries, and traceability.

My background spans over two decades in regulated environments, including pharmaceutical engineering, commissioning, qualification and validation (CQV), and large-scale project delivery. I bring that discipline to human–AI interaction and workflow design.


---

## What I Work On

- AI-augmented workflow architecture
- Lifecycle-driven system design and deterministic interaction logic
- Governance, rules, and human approval boundaries
- Structured knowledge and controlled outputs

## Architectural Principles

- Governance before capability
- Explicit states over implicit assumptions
- Boundaries are features
- Determinism where it matters
- Human authority is never delegated silently
